# Yomi AI

**A free personal AI agent that asks before it acts.** → [getyomi.in](https://getyomi.in)

Yomi reads your mail, plans your day, drafts replies, runs tasks on a schedule and works across 1,500+ apps: Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Notion, Slack, GitHub, Linear and many more. Reading happens right away; anything that sends, posts, books or deletes waits for your **Yes**.

### Talk to Yomi where you already are

One conversation, everywhere:

- 🌐 **Web app** at [getyomi.in](https://getyomi.in), installable with notifications
- 📱 **Android app**, in testing: [join the testers](https://getyomi.in/test)
- 💬 **Telegram** · **Discord** · **Slack** · **iMessage** (beta)
- 🛠️ **Linear** and **GitHub**: mention Yomi on an issue to hand it work

### How it's built

Everything runs on Cloudflare: Workers, Durable Objects, D1, R2, Vectorize, Workers AI and Browser Run. The website and docs are in 68 languages.
